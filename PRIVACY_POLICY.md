Information may be processed to load websites and provide browser features such as tabs, back/forward navigation, refresh, home, Mobile / Tablet / Desktop view, downloads, browsing history, Picture-in-Picture, background playback, website notifications, and the floating menu. The app's Android package lists the permissions below. Actual use depends on the feature, Android version, and permissions granted by you.

## 3. How Information Is Used

- **Website Browsing:** load and display websites you choose to visit.
- **Browser Controls:** provide navigation, refresh, home, and display-mode controls.
- **Downloads, History & Tabs:** show download activity, browsing history, and restore your open tabs.
- **Playback & Alerts:** Picture-in-Picture, background play, and notifications you allow.
- **Protection & Errors:** optional ad blocking, certificate checks, and an error page that explains why a site did not load.
- **Developer Console:** an optional console (Eruda) you can turn on for the page you are viewing.

HayGo is not intended to request information unrelated to browser functions. This policy does not claim that websites visited through the browser are prevented from collecting information.

## 4. Local Data & Retention

Browsing history, download records, your open tabs, app settings (such as Background play and ad blocking), per-site notification choices, cookies, and website storage are kept on your device in the app's private storage or by Android's web-rendering component. This information is not uploaded by HayGo.

- You may be able to clear browsing history, downloads, cookies, or website data through the browser or Android settings, where those controls are available.
- Downloaded files may remain on your device until you delete them.
- Websites you visit may store or retain information on their own systems under their own policies.
- HayGo does not run its own server that stores your browsing history, downloads or tabs, so we do not keep such data about you.
- If the app crashes, an error report is shown on your device and stays there. It is shared only if you choose to copy or send it.
- Uninstalling HayGo removes its private data from your device.

## 8. Security

HayGo can open both HTTPS and HTTP websites. HTTPS encrypts data between your device and a website; plain HTTP does not, so anyone on the network may be able to read or change it. Prefer HTTPS sites, especially when you sign in or enter personal or payment details. Typed addresses without a prefix open over HTTPS, except addresses on your own network (such as a router), which open over HTTP. HTTPS does not guarantee that a website is legitimate or safe.

If a secure connection fails because of a certificate problem, HayGo stops the page and shows an error page. For addresses on your own network only, it may ask whether you want to continue.

No app, website, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device updated, manage permissions carefully, and avoid submitting sensitive information to websites you do not trust.

## 10. External Services

The app uses internet access to load websites. Websites, web content providers, and Android platform services operate independently and may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Any external service linked from a website is outside our direct control. HayGo itself also connects to the following services:

- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging:** delivers push notifications, including to the app while it is closed. The app subscribes to a general announcements topic and creates a device token so messages can reach your device. The token is given to a website only after you allow notifications for that website. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Google Firebase Realtime Database:** HayGo may read announcement and notification history from it. HayGo does not send your browsing history, downloads or tabs to it.
- **jsDelivr (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`):** if you turn on the developer console, its scripts are downloaded from this content delivery network and cached on your device. The request reveals your IP address to the CDN.
- **Fontshare (`api.fontshare.com`):** HayGo's error and offline pages may load a font from this service, which reveals your IP address to it.

These services act under their own terms and privacy policies.
